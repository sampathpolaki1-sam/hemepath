Educational Disclaimer & Boundaries

HemePath is an educational learning software designed specifically for MBBS students studying hematology and pathophysiology. It is not a diagnostic device, clinical decision-support tool, or individualized treatment advisor. Learners must not input personal medical records or use the application to treat patients.

✅ Content Review & Safety Checklist

✓ All 15 lessons structured from standard MBBS pathology references.
✓ Objective questions include distractor rationale to eliminate misleading guessing.
✓ AI Tutor is topic-restricted strictly to Anemia, Polycythemia, and RBC physiology.
✓ Textbook photo uploads do not retain identifiable personal medical information.
✓ Target hematocrit for Polycythemia Vera phlebotomy strictly aligned with CYTO-PV trial (<45%).
